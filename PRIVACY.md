# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

MetalParenIndent does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- The app and its Xcode Source Editor Extension work entirely on your Mac.
- Source code you format in Xcode, or paste into the app's preview, is processed only in memory on your device. It is never saved by the app or sent anywhere.
- The app does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
- The app does not make any network connections.

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue in this repository.
